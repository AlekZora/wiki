# North Star

## The Goal

Build a personal AI assistant — one that genuinely understands me, my work, and my priorities — and use the revenue and credibility from smaller AI and game projects to fund serious space exploration work.

## The Path

1. **Smaller AI projects** — tools, agents, and products that generate revenue and sharpen my craft
2. **Game projects** — creative work that builds audience, skills, and funding
3. **Personal AI assistant** — the culmination: a system that knows my context, accelerates my thinking, and compounds over time
4. **Space exploration** — the end goal; everything else is in service of getting here

## Current Projects

### Side Quest AI — Architecture Proof of Concept

The side quest AI system is not just a game project. It is the first proof of concept for the core architecture that underlies the personal AI assistant and eventually autonomous space systems:

- **World state tracking** → spacecraft environment model
- **Constraint-based planning** → autonomous mission planning
- **Simulator layer** → genuine novel environment reasoning
- **Persistent memory** → assistant that knows your context

The game is the simplest domain to prove the architecture works. The architecture scales to every subsequent stage of the mission.

## Why This Order

Space exploration requires resources, credibility, and deep technical capability. The smaller projects aren't detours — they're the engine. Each shipped product builds the skills, network, and capital that make the bigger bets possible.

## Principles

- Ship something real, even if small
- Every project should teach something that transfers forward
- Don't optimize for prestige; optimize for compounding progress
- The assistant is a tool, not the goal — space is the goal
