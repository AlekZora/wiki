## Summary

An industry research note reporting MIDiA's Q2 2021 consumer survey on how people
discover new games. Social media feeds and personal recommendations tied for the top
discovery channel at 25% each, followed by social media ads (19%) and YouTube/Twitch
ads (12%); traditional media reviews fell to the bottom (6–7%), and Discord/podcasts
scored lowest for discovery specifically (2%/1%) despite being important for
retention and fandom among existing players. Discovery channel mix varies by engagement
tier: the highest-spending "Games Aficionados" lean more on social feeds (49%) than
personal recommendations (40%), while lower-spend "Forgotten Fans" lean slightly more
on personal recommendations.

## Key Points

- Word of mouth (as personal recommendation) has been overtaken/matched by algorithmic
  social feeds as a discovery channel — the two are now roughly equal in share, not WOM
  dominant.
- Discovery channel and engagement/fandom channel are different: Discord and podcasts
  score almost zero for *discovery* but remain important for community-building among
  people who already play.
- Discovery mix is segment-dependent — the most engaged, highest-spending players
  discover differently (feed-led) than more casual, lower-spend players
  (recommendation-led).

## My Take

This is the primary data source both the "How Single-Player and Multiplayer Games
Spread" synthesis and the Black Myth: Wukong dissemination study lean on — it's the
empirical anchor rather than a novel framework itself. The AI-relevant reframe: what
this shows is that "word of mouth" as a category has partially been absorbed into
algorithmic feed curation — the feed is now doing socially-mediated recommendation at
scale, blending peer signal with engagement-optimized ranking. That blurring is exactly
the dynamic to watch as AI-generated or AI-curated content enters the same feeds: the
distinction between "a friend recommended this" and "the algorithm surfaced this
because people like me engaged with it" is already eroding, and generative content
recommendation will erode it further.
