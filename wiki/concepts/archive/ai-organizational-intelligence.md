## Definition

The idea that a company's internal information flows — its Slack messages, emails, pull requests, meetings, documents — can be unified into a queryable AI "world model" that replaces the information-relay function of traditional management hierarchy. Separately, the recognition that AI has crossed a threshold where companies can sell work (outcomes) rather than tools (copilots), transforming software businesses into services businesses.

## How I Think About It

Two complementary shifts happening at once:

**Internal:** Jack Dorsey's argument that hierarchy exists to relay information through layers of management — and AI can now do that job better. The company becomes a "mini-AGI" where all artifacts flow into a central intelligence anyone can query, and humans at the edge provide judgment and alignment rather than acting as information relays. The three-role model (IC/builder, DRI/owner, player-coach) strips out middle management.

**External:** Sequoia's argument that the shift from copilot (tool + professional) to autopilot (outcome sold directly) is transforming the business model. The wedge is always outsourced, intelligence-heavy work where a budget line exists. Over time, as AI accumulates domain data, today's judgement work becomes tomorrow's intelligence work.

The two ideas are linked: companies rebuilding internally around AI intelligence layers are also those best positioned to sell outcomes externally, because they understand what AI can and can't do at depth.
