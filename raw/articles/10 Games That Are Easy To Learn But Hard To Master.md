The feeling of enjoying a new game right away can be hard to beat, especially since many games have long or complex tutorials that delay the gratification of beating a level. However, one of the best features of easy-to-learn games is how they manage to crank up the difficulty by asking for precision and speed from their players.

Challenging a player’s reflexes and dedication to improvement can add hours to a game’s playtime because it encourages them to come back for another try. By combining the feeling of progress with a compelling plot, lovable characters, or a fantastic soundtrack, these seemingly simple games have earned a following for their surprising difficulty.

## 10 Tetris

![Tetrominoes from Tetris](https://static0.gamerantimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Tetris-Blocks_Tetris.jpg?q=50&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=1.5)

Tetrominoes from Tetris

When it comes to puzzle games, *[Tetris](https://gamerant.com/tag/tetris/)* is among the most well-known for its four-block tetrominoes and memorable theme song. Players organize the tetrominoes into lines to clear space, with the occasional opportunity to hold an inconvenient piece until there’s a better spot.

*Tetris* not only gets faster and requires players to clear more lines to complete a level, but there are also techniques*,* like T-Spins or other twists, that players can learn to improve their game. Since there are seemingly endless ways to score points in [games like *Tetris*,](https://gamerant.com/best-puzzle-games-like-tetris/) it’s a refreshing challenge each time.

## 9 Super Monkey Ball

![Ai Ai from Super Monkey Ball chasing after a banana](https://static0.gamerantimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/monkey-ball.jpg?q=50&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=1.5)

In most 3D games, players control characters and move them around levels to reach a goal. However, many *[Super Monkey Ball](https://gamerant.com/tag/super-monkey-ball/)* games turn this idea on its head by making players tilt the stages rather than move the playable character directly. The tilt’s angle and duration impact how quickly the characters move to pick up bananas and reach the goal.

The game also has a timer, so players must respond without overcompensating for the stage’s movements. *Super Monkey Ball* sets its players up for success in its first few stages with stable platforms and wide pathways, but as the game continues, players have to overcome thin, moving levels with twists, turns, and obstacles.

## 8 Mega Man

![Mega Man and two other characters from Mega Man Battle Network 5 ](https://static0.gamerantimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/megaman-battle-network-5-cropped.jpg?q=50&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=1.5)

Starting up a *[Mega Man](https://gamerant.com/tag/mega-man/)* game for the first time offers a customizable experience that takes both strategy and skill. Players can conquer bosses in whichever order they see fit, since, generally, the games begin with all but a handful of their levels unlocked.

However, a fair share of *Mega Man’s* difficulty lies in its freedom. Defeating the bosses in a particular order can be beneficial since [Mega Man learns new skills](https://gamerant.com/game-characters-can-transform-their-bodies-into-weapons/) after taking down each enemy, but determining the best plan of attack is one of the first challenges that players face.

## 7 Pid

![Kurt using his gravity powers to avoid enemies](https://static0.gamerantimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/pid-cropped.jpg?q=50&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=1.5)

*Pid* is [a puzzle platformer](https://gamerant.com/hardest-puzzle-games/) by Might and Delight, in which a young boy named Kurt finds himself on another planet with the power to manipulate gravity and must use this power to make his way back home. With a charming soundtrack playing in the background, Kurt uses his powers to defeat enemies and bosses with unique designs.

Despite *Pid’s* inviting style and story, Kurt’s powers require precise timing and direction to make it through complex levels and battles. Strategic use of the surroundings and confident actions are essential to make it back home.

## 6 Pac-Man

![Pac-Man and the ghosts from Pac-Man](https://static0.gamerantimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/pac-man-arcade-cabinet-logo-ghosts.jpg?q=50&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=1.5)

The arcade classic, *Pac-Man*, has a simple goal. Players must direct Pac-Man through a maze to eat pellets and avoid ghosts that chase him. [*Pac-Man*](https://gamerant.com/tag/pac-man/) doesn’t have tricky controls, but it gets progressively more complex as it goes on since the ghosts can speed up and have particular behaviors that are essential to understand for strategizing.

The original game features hundreds of levels for players to overcome, but the final stage in the game, level 256, cannot be completed because of a glitch that disrupts half of the screen. Despite this, [reaching level 256 of *Pac-Man*](https://gamerant.com/pac-man-facts-trivia/) is an accomplishment that takes tremendous dedication.

## 5 Guitar Hero

![Characters playing a song by Rush](https://static0.gamerantimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/guitar-hero-2-yyz.jpg?q=50&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=1.5)

Like many other successful rhythm games, *Guitar Hero* is well-known because it brings players together to recreate [some of the greatest hits](https://gamerant.com/best-songs-to-play-guitar-hero-2/) while jamming out by pressing color-correlated buttons. The game itself is easy enough to pick up, but the same cannot be said of some of the tracks it contains.

[*Guitar Hero*](https://gamerant.com/tag/guitar-hero/) has different difficulty settings for players of all skill levels, but some of the game’s challenges come with the genre. Timing, speed, and complex patterns make certain songs hard, even while being played on easier difficulties. Perfecting the quickest, most energetic jams in a rhythm game takes time and a clear understanding of the game’s seemingly simple mechanics.

## 4 Cuphead

![Cuphead and Mugman in front of King Dice and the Devil from Cuphead](https://static0.gamerantimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/cuphead-key-art.jpg?q=50&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=1.5)

*Cuphead* stands out from other run-and-gun platformers for its smooth classic cartoon aesthetics and hectic boss battles [with hard-to-match difficulty](https://gamerant.com/platformers-harder-than-cuphead/). Despite the focus and dedication that the game requires, the controls seem easy to learn at first. Cuphead has to run, jump, parry, and shoot through various stages, beating bosses along the way.

It is a classic video game plot. Still, once it jumps into its story, the sheer amount of differences between the bosses and levels can be startling for some players. [*Cuphead*](https://gamerant.com/tag/cuphead/) takes tremendous time and adaptability to complete and considerably more to fully master.

## 3 Crash Bandicoot

![Box art from Crash Bandicoot N Sane Trilogy](https://static0.gamerantimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/crash-bandicoot-n-sane-trilogy-box-art.jpg?q=50&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=1.5)

Similar to *Cuphead,* the games in the *[Crash Bandicoot](https://gamerant.com/tag/crash-bandicoot/)* series are primarily platformers. Players take control of Crash, a bandicoot who attempts to thwart Doctor Neo Cortex’s plans of world domination by spinning, sliding, and slamming into enemies. As Crash makes his way through levels, he must also try to break boxes and collect gems.

Although the *Crash Bandicoot* games have many objectives and boss battles, [the most difficult stages](https://gamerant.com/hardest-most-difficult-crash-bandicoot-games/) make players time their movements carefully and intentionally with intense climbs and a surprising variety of enemies.

## 2 Tekken

![Tekken 7](https://static0.gamerantimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/yyy-1.jpg?q=50&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=1.5)

Fighting games, like *[Tekken](https://gamerant.com/tag/tekken/),* are perfect for button-mashing and beginners. When it comes to tournaments though, fans of the genre show just how important and impressive learning a character’s techniques can be.

Since the series has such a wide roster of characters, the *Tekken* games are some of the [hardest to learn](https://gamerant.com/hardest-fighting-games/). Still, the impressive hand-to-hand combat inspires fans to dedicate time to the series. After all, in intense matches, back blocking and special moves at the right time are essential to winning.

## 1 Celeste

![Celeste cover](https://static0.gamerantimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Celeste.jpg?q=50&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=1.5)

*Celeste* isan [indie platformer success story](https://gamerant.com/indie-games-major-success-stories/) about a trans woman named Madeline who decides to climb up Celeste Mountain to clear her mind and challenge her self-doubt. As she climbs and dashes around the mountain, she has to overcome spikes, falling objects, and moving platforms that become more troublesome the higher she climbs.

Since the climb isn’t as simple as it first seems, the game features an auto function that players can use if they need it. Despite this, Madeline’s journey has a touching and compelling story, giving players a reason to persevere through its difficulties. What's more, it's an incredibly intuitive title, with the game rarely needing to explain new mechanics whenever they are introduced.
