Jack Dorsey (Block CEO) and Roelof Botha (Sequoia partner and Block board member) join to discuss a bold claim [they wrote about recently](https://sequoiacap.com/article/from-hierarchy-to-intelligence/): the traditional corporate hierarchy isn’t just inefficient — it’s obsolete. Jack made one of the toughest calls in recent business history: cutting 40% of his workforce and rebuilding the company from the ground up around what he calls an AI “intelligence layer.” Jack breaks down his vision for simplifying into just three roles, and what it means to replace a pyramid org chart with a circle — AI at the center, and people at the edge. Roelof shares his perspective on how AI-native startups are building differently, and what CEO qualities are timeless.
